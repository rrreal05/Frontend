Welcome to the countryside

Located near Etisalat on Khalid Bin Sultan Street in Al Sanaiya, French Country Side Cafe has been a favourite chill-out spot in Al Ain for years.

Enjoy the relaxed outdoor seating, garden-style family area, and the best double-apple and grape-mint shisha in town — all in a cool, welcoming atmosphere.
